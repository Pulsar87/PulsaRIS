Fee Component Guide
Professional Component (26):

Covers the radiologist's professional services including:

  • Image interpretation
  • Report generation
  • Consultation with referring physician
Technical Component (TC):

Covers facility costs including:

  • Imaging equipment
  • Technologist staff
  • Supplies and contrast
  • Facility overhead
Global Fee:

Combined professional + technical when both components are provided by the same entity.

Tip: If global fee is not specified, the system will automatically calculate it as Professional + Technical.
